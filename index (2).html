I can support you through menopause with coaching

Did you know that 1 in 10 women leave their jobs because of menopause? Or that menopause is cited as a cause in 73% of midlife divorces?

With the amount of change going on in your body through menopause, isn't it time you gave yourself the same attention we give to puberty and pregnancy? You owe it to yourself.

I will work with you through a powerful coaching plan developed by world renowned menopause coach Lauren Chiren of Women of a Certain Stage, that can transform your experience of menopause.

If brain fog has you losing words mid-sentence or forgetting why you walked into a room, you're not losing your mind — it's a recognised part of menopause, and there are practical techniques to work with it. Together, we'll also build your confidence back up and bring your anxiety down, so you feel like yourself again.

And support doesn't stop when the sessions end — you'll get follow-up check-ins to help what you've learned actually stick.

Six things you can do today to regain control

Coaching isn't just about how you feel — it's about practical tools that make a measurable difference to your symptoms.

Track

Notice your patterns

Keep a simple note of sleep, mood and energy for two weeks. Patterns show you what actually helps, rather than guessing.

Hydrate

Drink more than you think you need

Menopause blunts your sense of thirst. Even mild dehydration can make brain fog and hot flushes worse — so drink on a schedule, not just when you feel thirsty.

Routine

Anchor your morning and evening

A short, calm routine at either end of the day steadies your nervous system and improves sleep quality over time.

Move

Build strength and flexibility

Regular movement supports mood and energy now, and protects your bones and heart for the long term.

Boundaries

Protect your energy

Saying no where you can isn't indulgent — it's one of the most effective, practical tools you have for managing symptoms.

Support

Ask for the right help

At home and at work. A clear conversation about what you need is often the single biggest change you can make.

Does your business need a Menopause Action Plan? I can help

Are you unclear about what you currently offer employees experiencing menopause? Could you do with some strategies to develop your offering and meet the new guidelines and legislation?

From April 2027, businesses with 250+ employees are required to have a Menopause Action Plan. If you're an employer with under 250 employees, why not get ahead of the game?

It's not just about compliance. Retaining your experienced employees and decreasing sick leave makes good business sense. I can work with you on this.

  • I can work with you to audit your current provision
  • I can help you write your Action Plan
  • I can set up supportive partnerships in your organisation
  • I can train your managers to support staff with menopause symptoms
  • I can suggest adjustments that can be made to your working environment
  • I'll provide ongoing follow-up support to help your Action Plan stick, not just sit in a drawer
